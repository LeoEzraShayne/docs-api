- New Project creation page

  /app/new

# Current Problem

The New Project form fields are too unclear.

Users may not know what to enter, especially for:

- 業界
- システム種別
- 目的
- 対象範囲
- 議事録

Example:
For 「システム種別」, users may not know whether to enter:
- Androidアプリ
- Webアプリ
- macOSアプリ
- 業務システム
- 管理画面
- ECサイト
- SaaS

The form needs better placeholders and compact guidance.

---

# Important UX Policy

Do NOT add large helper text under every field.
Do NOT show example lists under every field.
Do NOT make the form visually noisy.

Use placeholders as the main guidance method.

Only these fields may have visible compact examples or small gray helper text:

1. 業界
2. システム種別
3. 目的
4. 対象範囲
5. 議事録

All other fields should use placeholder text only.

If helper text is used, keep it:
- Small
- Gray
- One short sentence
- Not visually dominant

If example chips are used, keep them:
- Small
- Limited to 4 to 8 chips
- One line or compact wrapping
- Only for the five fields listed above

---

Replace it with natural Japanese:
